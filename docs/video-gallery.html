Headline failure exhibit

Starburst crystallization, lead exhibit headline Seed image: starburst.png (a non-Minecraft optical-illusion image)

starburst-crystallization.mp4

Out-of-distribution starburst seed fed to the live server with normal WASD + mouse input. A reasonable failure mode would be the model producing chaotic-but-Minecraft-shaped block soup that at least drifts under input. Instead the model locks on the starburst and stays there. This is the cleanest single-clip demonstration of the architectural ceiling.
